    Method

    How the desk reads the day.

    01

    Collect

    Every four hours the desk pulls the latest reporting from public RSS and Atom feeds, and reads the reporting in each entry, not just the headline.

    02

    Retrieve

    Your question is matched against passages from this edition using vector search. The model sees the reporting, not a general memory of the world.

    03

    Answer

    The reply is written from those passages and cites each source by number. If the edition does not cover something, the desk says so.
